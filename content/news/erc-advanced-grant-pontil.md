Massimiliano Pontil, principal investigator of CSML, has been awarded a European Research Council Advanced Grant for LEO. The project aims to build machine learning algorithms that are efficient, reliable and interpretable, and that can incorporate the laws of physics to predict the long-term behaviour of complex systems such as climate dynamics. Advanced Grants support researchers with an established track record of significant achievements over the past decade.
