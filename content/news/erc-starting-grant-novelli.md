Pietro Novelli, a researcher in CSML, has been awarded a European Research Council Starting Grant of €1.5 million over five years. The project, LINDY, will develop new AI algorithms that break down complex dynamical systems, such as climate evolution or protein behaviour, into simpler, linear building blocks. Novelli will carry out the research at IIT's labs in Genoa and Pisa, building his own research group.
