Lisa Cuneo is a postdoctoral researcher at IIT working across the Molecular Microscopy and Spectroscopy and CSML research lines. Her background is in applied mathematics, with a focus on inverse problems, biomedical data analysis and machine learning.

She holds a PhD in Physics and Nanosciences and has completed research stays at Aalto University and the University of Cambridge. Her work connects advanced optical microscopy with computational modelling, developing inverse-problem, optimization and machine-learning methods that improve the interpretability, resolution and quantitative power of microscopy data.
