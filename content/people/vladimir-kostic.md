Vladimir R. Kostić is an Associate Professor of Applied Mathematics at the University of Novi Sad, Serbia, and a researcher collaborating with the Computational Statistics and Machine Learning group at the Italian Institute of Technology, Genoa. He develops computational methods that combine numerical linear algebra, statistics, and machine learning, with a particular interest in stochastic processes and dynamical systems and their application to AI for science.

He earned a PhD in Numerical Mathematics from the University of Novi Sad in 2010, and has held postdoctoral and visiting positions at the Technical University of Berlin, IIT Genoa, and École Polytechnique.
