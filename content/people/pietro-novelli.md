Pietro Novelli is a researcher in the Computational Statistics and Machine Learning unit and the Principal Investigator of the ERC Starting Grant LINDY. His main research efforts concern learning dynamical systems and developing machine-learning tools for the physical sciences, including reinforcement learning and representation learning, with applications to atomistic simulations.

He was previously a PhD student at the Scuola Normale Superiore, where he studied many-body effects in the transport and optical properties of 2D materials.
