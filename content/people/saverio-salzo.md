Saverio Salzo received an MSc in Pure Mathematics from the University of Bari in 2001 and a PhD in Computer Science from the University of Genoa in 2012. His research interests include nonsmooth optimization, proximal splitting and stochastic algorithms, hyperparameter and bilevel optimization, optimization in probability spaces, support vector machines in Banach spaces and tensor kernel methods.

He was a visiting scholar at KU Leuven and has been an honorary lecturer at University College London since 2020. He has held a tenured assistant professorship at Sapienza University of Rome since July 2020.
