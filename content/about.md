---
title: About CSML
---
Our research spans machine learning theory and algorithms, drawing on mathematics and statistics such as empirical processes and numerical optimization. We develop efficient, reliable and interpretable methods for learning from complex real-world data.

Our work connects foundational machine learning with scientific discovery, computational physics and numerical simulation. Current directions include time-dependent data, transfer and reinforcement learning, uncertainty quantification, operator learning and the discovery of dynamical systems from data.
